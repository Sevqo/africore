# Odoo Community HR, Attendance and Time Off — focused reference

Question: which model-level checks and workflows matter when comparing AfriCore People? This note records source observations at Odoo commit `9d233dca4156d58ca573e9cdc3b32327c97aa282` (branch `20.0`). The inspected manifests declare LGPL-3. No Odoo source or asset is copied.

## Addons and observed behavior

- `addons/hr/__manifest__.py` declares Employees as an application, with identity, resource-calendar, and hierarchy dependencies, HR security, employee, department and settings views. `addons/hr/models/hr_employee.py` defines employee, manager and department relationships and visibility-sensitive fields; `addons/hr/models/hr_department.py` supplies department hierarchy behavior. Employee and department records are HR-domain records, not payroll by themselves.
- `addons/hr/views/hr_employee_views.xml` composes employee form, list, kanban, hierarchy, activity, graph and pivot views and the employee action. `addons/hr/views/hr_department_views.xml` defines department form/list/kanban/hierarchy and actions. `addons/hr/security/hr_security.xml` and `security/ir.access.csv` define the HR access boundary.
- `addons/hr_attendance/__manifest__.py` declares a separate Attendances application layered on `hr` and work-entry/time-rule components. `addons/hr_attendance/models/hr_attendance.py` validates ordered check-in/check-out, break duration, one open attendance, and overlap (`_check_validity_check_in_check_out`, `_check_break_duration`, `_check_validity`). `addons/hr_attendance/views/hr_attendance_view.xml` provides attendance list, form, search and action patterns. `tests/test_hr_attendance_constraints.py` covers reversed intervals, open attendance and overlaps; access tests are in `tests/test_hr_attendance_access_rights.py`.
- `addons/hr_holidays/__manifest__.py` declares Time Off and its approval/calendar dependencies. `addons/hr_holidays/models/hr_leave.py` defines request states and separate approval, refusal, cancellation and return-to-approval transitions (`action_approve`, `action_refuse`, `action_cancel`, `action_back_to_approval`). `_check_date` rejects inconsistent periods and conflicting leave; `_check_approval_update` gates transitions by current authority. `addons/hr_holidays/views/hr_leave_views.xml` and `views/hr_holidays_views.xml` define request, list, form, search and action views; `tests/test_leave_requests.py` covers overlapping requests, insufficient allocations and access checks.
- No `addons/hr_contract` directory exists in this Community clone. Employee version and contract-template records visible in HR are not evidence of a complete contract/payroll application. No Enterprise-only capability is assumed.

## AfriCore comparison

AfriCore owns employee profiles, department records, employer policy, private HR visibility, approval snapshots, leave balances, attendance and retained corrections in its People service (`src/erp/people-*.ts`). Its React workspace is `website/src/apps/PeopleWorkspace.tsx`, with forms/actions in `PeopleEditors.tsx`, `PeopleManagement.tsx`, and `PeopleRecordActions.tsx`. Service routes are registered through `src/erp/routes.ts`. The existing service is deliberately narrower: leave accrual follows explicit employer policy and integer thousandths, compensation remains private record data, and no payroll, statutory deduction, biometric or location claim is made.

Frontend integration gaps found in this checkpoint: the workspace originally loaded configuration, employees, Team, departments, leave types, leave, attendance and approvals in one `Promise.all`. A failure in any auxiliary service hid successful employee/configuration results. The employee detail view also fetched leave balances before compensation in one chain, and policy failure could leave the initial load spinning without recovery. These paths now apply responses independently, keep successful records visible, report the failing source and provide a People data retry. Backend authorization, exact-version approval and attendance correction rules remain authoritative.

## Phase 2.10B expansion and current parity

AfriCore adds these persisted resource kinds to the existing encrypted People authority rather than introducing another employee database: positions, reusable skills, private employee profiles, resume entries, employee skill records, certifications, recruitment openings/candidates/applications, goals, appraisals, lifecycle plans and lifecycle cases. `people-expansion-schema.ts` adds one organization/environment-scoped index; migration `021-people-expansion` is additive and has not been applied to a hosted or production database. The implementation and route list are in `src/erp/people-expansion.ts`, `src/erp/people-expansion-schema.ts`, `src/erp/routes.ts` and `src/erp/metadata.ts`.

Employee changes include a position catalog linked to department and hiring manager, required skill metadata, responsibilities, category and planned vacancies; a reusable skill catalog with proficiency and HR verification; dated resume/experience metadata; certification metadata and expiry windows; and HR-only private profile fields. The workspace adds profile sections, employee cards/list filters, lifecycle screens and organization chart navigation. Private-profile reads remain HR-only; personal resume/self-assessment visibility follows self-service policy. This is still an embedded employee detail section, not the requested complete standalone employee document with tabs, avatar and all related-record smart buttons. Resume and certification attachment links, language-specific resume categories and a full editable history UI are not implemented here; documents continue to use the separate Documents service.

The organization chart is now a recursive, searchable, expand/collapse manager hierarchy with profile navigation. Its API returns only currently visible employment records and explicitly marks a manager outside that visibility set without identifying them. It is capped at 5,000 visible employees. It does not yet group by department, show direct-report counts, add zoom/fit controls or lazy-load large trees. Department parent cycles are rejected; an active department cannot be archived while active employees remain assigned.

Time Off adds a period-bounded leave/holiday register with current permission-aware names and status-only views for other employees. The existing leave service continues to own requests, approval, balance adjustments and history. The new calendar is a date-range list, not a month/week/day grid; this expansion does not add a separate allocation approval register or new allocation state machine. Configured weekday, holiday and employer timezone rules remain in the current People policy, and prior policy revisions are not reconstructed for historical attendance.

Attendance adds an HR analysis endpoint with date range, employee/status filters, pagination, recorded seconds, open sessions, missing-checkout review flags and current configured work-hours checks. Flags are for human review only. The service does not infer overtime or absence, and there are no per-employee effective-dated schedules, approved extra-hours rules, historical schedule versions, grouping controls or location/biometric proof. Without configured work hours the endpoint says schedule-based checks are unavailable.

Recruitment adds encrypted candidates, job openings, recruiter assignment and a validated application-stage transition path. A deliberate, idempotent hire creates one canonical employee after employee-number and duplicate checks; linking a workspace login is optional and separately admin-authorized. There is no public job publishing, candidate messaging, calendar invitation, external resume ingestion or recruiter portal. Interview dates and assessments are recorded metadata only.

Goals support progress and optional skill/Work task references. Appraisals support scheduled/in-progress/review/completed states, self-assessment, manager feedback and redacted private reviewer notes. Recruitment and performance delegation are separate from HR and compensation access. The local Odoo 20 Community checkout does not include `hr_appraisal`; the supplied goal/appraisal screenshots are behavioral references, not proof of Community implementation or Enterprise parity.

The HR-only overview and report endpoints aggregate totals on the server, not from the visible employee page: current employee status counts, active employees by department, period hires/reviews/lifecycle completions, openings, applicants, leave requests pending approval, employees currently on approved leave, open attendance sessions, missing-checkout review count, pending reviews and certification expiry metadata. Counts use the selected employer-local period where applicable; certificate expiry is a forward 90-day metadata window. Compensation is excluded. Offboarding is represented by a checklist and becomes `inactive` only when completed; there is no separate `departing` or leave-of-absence employment state.

All new records are organization/environment scoped, versioned and idempotent on writes. Role checks remain service-side: HR, compensation, recruitment and performance are distinct; candidate-to-employee conversion does not create a workspace login; employee visibility does not confer Work, Documents or other service rights. Existing Documents attachments, approval records, and Work task creation remain owned by their original services. No payroll/statutory calculation, external notification, biometric attendance, or location tracking is added.

## AfriCore dependency and workflow audit

| Workflow | Authoritative service and API | Required configuration / selectors | Permission and integrity checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee directory/profile | `people-profiles.ts`; `/people/configuration`, `/people/employees`, `/people/departments`, employee detail/history | Review employer policy first; choose current workspace member and existing department from selectors | HR role and manager-scoped projections are rechecked by service; employee identity does not create workspace membership; versioned writes and retained history |
| Leave request/review | `people-leave.ts`, approvals service; `/people/leave-types`, employee `/leave-preview`, `/leave`, `/leave-balances`, `/business/approvals`, `/approvals/:id/decide` | Active employer-defined leave type, dates, current separate reviewer, configured work week/holidays | Service validates policy/version, eligibility, balance, date overlap and current approver authority; decisions are separate, reasoned and idempotent |
| Attendance/correction | `people-attendance.ts`; `/people/attendance`, employee `/check-in`, `/check-out`, attendance `/:id/correct` | Linked employee identity for self check-in/out; reason and authorized reviewer for correction | Current self-write or HR permission; one open attendance, ordered intervals, overlap checks; corrections retain superseded history |
| Restricted compensation | `people-profiles.ts`; employee `/compensation` | Existing employee; no raw IDs surfaced as user-entered values | Separate compensation permission is checked on each request; compensation is stored as private terms, not payroll calculation |
| Reports/onboarding | `people-reports.ts`, `people-onboarding.ts`; People report routes and employee `/onboarding-project` | Current source records; onboarding uses installed Work capability only | Aggregate service applies scoped visibility; onboarding creates operational Work tasks without copying private HR payloads |
| Workspace and service state | shared identity/Team and service activation; `/team`, People configuration/activation endpoints | Current organization, environment and members | All endpoints re-resolve actor, membership and service state; UI visibility is not an authorization boundary |

The directory is server-searchable and paginated; endpoints that intentionally cap leave, attendance or leave-type history return `has_more`/`truncated` and a limit or notice. The UI labels capped coverage. Selectors are populated from authorized `/team`, department and leave-type records rather than asking users for internal identifiers. On any read failure, the UI now distinguishes the failed source from a legitimate empty list and exposes a People data retry; policy failure is surfaced in the initial load state rather than leaving an endless spinner.

## Supplied visual-reference findings and runtime status

The supplied Odoo 19 screenshots show employee cards, private-information and resume tabs, an expandable department chart, Time Off calendar and allocations, attendance grouping, and a goal form. They were used as composition and workflow references; Odoo 20 Community source behavior takes precedence. AfriCore now has employee cards/list, an expandable reporting hierarchy, profile subsections, a period-based leave register, attendance analysis, recruitment, lifecycle and performance/report panels. Major visual differences remain, including the embedded profile instead of a full-page tabbed employee record, list rows instead of a month/week/day leave grid, and list-based rather than calendar- or kanban-based recruitment.

The running AfriCore People interface was not captured side by side at matching viewport sizes in this checkpoint, so visual parity is not claimed. The local API was stopped during runtime triage (no listener on port 5000). The previously reported authenticated Work Timesheets/Reporting screen still showed “Resource not found”; this People implementation does not resolve that separate runtime issue. Founder browser, role and responsive acceptance remain pending.

This is a focused Community source investigation, not an exhaustive audit of all HR addons, access-rule XML, payroll localization, time-rule adapters, browser tours or every model method. The Odoo source is prior art; AfriCore's contracts and safeguards remain authoritative.

## 2026-11-19 follow-up — recruitment and private employment fields

Focused follow-up at the then-current local Odoo 20 Community HEAD `bd181ee5eb1f200db3c062733d4f464c37ef9e25` (branch `20.0`): `addons/hr/models/hr_employee.py` delegates effective employment facts to `hr.version` and marks private fields with HR-only groups. `addons/hr_recruitment/models/hr_applicant.py` keeps applicant data separate while providing a deliberate optional employee relation, job, stage, recruiter, and hire-date fields. `addons/hr_recruitment/models/hr_recruitment_stage.py` marks hired stages explicitly. The `create_employee_from_applicant` action and focused test in `addons/hr_recruitment/tests/test_recruitment.py` demonstrate an explicit transition to an employee. The pinned Community tree has no `addons/hr_appraisal`; this does not describe Enterprise Appraisals.

AfriCore adaptation: preserve separate encrypted candidate/applicant and employee records, use an explicit duplicate-checked transition, and keep employee/login links independent. Private profile and reviewer-only fields remain server-redacted. This observation does not authorize external job publishing, candidate email, workspace account changes, or a payroll/contract inference. See research log entry 2026-11-19 and decision D-009.
